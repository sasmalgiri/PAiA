## Required (priority 1) — life insurance product taxonomy

- **Term life** — level (10/15/20/25/30 yr), convertible, ROP (return
  of premium), guaranteed renewable, decreasing term
- **Permanent life**:
  - Whole Life (participating dividend-paying, non-participating,
    limited pay 10/15/20-pay, single premium)
  - Universal Life (UL — flexible premium, accumulation focus)
  - Indexed Universal Life (IUL — cap / participation rate / spread,
    index strategies, NAIC AG 49 illustration constraints)
  - Variable Universal Life (VUL — separate accounts, requires Series 6
    or 7 license, securities product)
- **Modified Endowment Contract (MEC)** — 7-pay test, distribution
  taxation consequences

Include needs-analysis methods: DIME (Debt, Income, Mortgage, Education),
human-life-value, capital-retention.

## Required (priority 1) — long-term care insurance

- **Traditional standalone LTC** — daily/monthly benefit, benefit
  period, elimination period, inflation rider (compound vs simple)
- **Hybrid life-LTC** — chronic illness rider, LTC rider on permanent
  policy, 7702B vs 101(g) tax treatment
- **Hybrid annuity-LTC** — Pension Protection Act of 2006 tax-free
  growth into LTC benefits
- **LTC Partnership Programs** by state — Medicaid asset disregard
- **Inflation considerations** — 3% / 5% compound critical for
  multi-decade need

## Required (priority 1) — disability income insurance

- **Own-occupation vs Any-occupation** vs **Modified own-occ**
- **Residual / partial disability** benefits
- **Future-Increase Option (FIO)** / **Benefit Update Rider**
- **COLA rider** during claim
- **Group LTD vs individual** — typically 60% gross / 40% net taxable
  for employer-paid; 60% gross / 60% net tax-free for employee-paid
- **Business Overhead Expense (BOE)** for self-employed
- **Key Person Disability** for businesses
- **Definitions matter** — "true own-occ" vs "specialty-language" —
  flag for medical professionals

## Required (priority 1) — annuity product taxonomy

- **Single Premium Immediate Annuity (SPIA)** — period certain, life
  with period certain, joint-and-survivor variants
- **Deferred Income Annuity (DIA)** — longevity insurance, QLAC
- **Multi-Year Guaranteed Annuity (MYGA)** — fixed rate for term,
  MVA (market value adjustment), surrender charges
- **Fixed Indexed Annuity (FIA)** — cap rate, participation rate,
  spread, index-strategy menu, illustration testing
- **Variable Annuity (VA)** — separate accounts, GLWB (guaranteed
  lifetime withdrawal benefit), GMIB, GMAB, GMDB
- **Registered Index-Linked Annuity (RILA / Structured Annuity)** —
  buffer or floor, 6-yr or longer term
- **Qualified Longevity Annuity Contract (QLAC)** — IRA only, $200k
  cap (2024), defers RMD on QLAC portion to age 85
