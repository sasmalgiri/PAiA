## Required (priority 1) — federal transfer tax

- **IRC Subtitle B Estate and Gift Tax** selected sections — §§2001
  (estate tax imposed), 2010 (unified credit), 2031 (gross estate),
  2032 / 2032A (alternate valuation, special-use valuation), 2033
  (property in which decedent had interest), 2056 (marital deduction
  / QDOT for non-citizen spouse), 2503 (gift tax annual exclusion),
  2522 (charitable transfers deduction), 2601-2664 (GST tax)
- **IRC §1014** — step-up in basis on inheritance
- **IRC §691** — Income in respect of decedent (IRD)
- **IRS Form 706** instructions (federal estate tax return)
- **IRS Form 709** instructions (gift / GST tax return)
- **IRS Pub 559** — Survivors, Executors, Administrators
- **IRS Pub 950** — Introduction to Estate and Gift Taxes
- **Federal estate exemption amounts** by year, including TCJA
  ~50% sunset post-2025 (currently $13.61M 2024 / $13.99M 2025,
  reverts to ~$7M+ in 2026 unless extended)

## Required (priority 1) — state estate / inheritance tax matrix

One file per state with current threshold, top rate, portability
status, and effective date. States with estate tax (as of 2024):

- **Connecticut** — fully phased out by 2023, no state estate tax
- **District of Columbia** — $4.7M exemption (2024)
- **Hawaii** — $5.49M exemption
- **Illinois** — $4M exemption
- **Maine** — $6.8M exemption
- **Maryland** — $5M exemption (+ separate inheritance tax)
- **Massachusetts** — $2M exemption (raised from $1M in 2023)
- **Minnesota** — $3M exemption
- **New York** — $6.94M exemption (with cliff: exceed by 5% = full estate taxed)
- **Oregon** — $1M exemption
- **Rhode Island** — $1.77M (2024) exemption
- **Vermont** — $5M exemption
- **Washington** — $2.193M exemption

States with inheritance tax: Iowa (phasing out), Kentucky, Maryland,
Nebraska, New Jersey, Pennsylvania — each has different beneficiary-
class exemptions.

Update annually — these are moving targets.

## Required (priority 1) — trust types and uses

- **Revocable Living Trust** — probate avoidance, incapacity planning
- **Pour-Over Will** — companion to RLT
- **Irrevocable Life Insurance Trust (ILIT)** — three-year rule,
  Crummey powers, life insurance estate-removal
- **Spousal Lifetime Access Trust (SLAT)** — reciprocal-trust trap,
  use-it-or-lose-it pre-2026 sunset planning
- **Grantor Retained Annuity Trust (GRAT)** — zeroed-out GRAT,
  rolling GRATs, 7520 rate interplay
- **Intentionally Defective Grantor Trust (IDGT)** — installment sale
  to IDGT, defective grantor status for income tax
- **Charitable Remainder Trust (CRT)** — CRUT (unitrust) vs CRAT
  (annuity trust), 10% minimum remainder
- **Charitable Lead Trust (CLAT)** — front-loaded charitable
  deduction, post-charity remainder to family
- **Qualified Personal Residence Trust (QPRT)**
- **Domestic Asset Protection Trust (DAPT)** — state-by-state
  availability (NV, DE, SD, AK, others)
- **Special Needs Trust** — first-party (d4A, payback to state for
  Medicaid) vs third-party (no payback)
- **ABLE account** — disability-account companion
