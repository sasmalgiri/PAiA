# Paralegal content slot — SEC + FTC Guidance

Drop the following primary-source documents in this directory before
publishing PAiA Legal v1.0.0:

- SEC Regulation S-K + S-X key items
- SEC Staff Accounting Bulletins (recent)
- SEC C&DIs on disclosure topics
- 1933 Act §§ 4(a)(2), Reg D Rules 504-506
- HSR Act + Premerger Notification Rules
- FTC Merger Guidelines (2023)
- DOJ Antitrust Division business reviews
- FTC consent orders and analysis (recent settlements)

All sources are public US government works.

The pack installer ingests every file in this directory.
