# Paralegal content slot — Contract Templates

Drop annotated commercial, employment, licensing, and NDA templates in
this directory before publishing PAiA Legal v1.0.0. Suggested set:

- Mutual NDA (US-CA + US-NY)
- Unilateral NDA (employer-favorable)
- Master Services Agreement
- Statement of Work template
- Software License Agreement (proprietary)
- Software License Agreement (open-source-friendly)
- Patent License Agreement
- Trademark License Agreement
- Offer letter (at-will US)
- Employment agreement (executive)
- Separation Agreement + Release
- Consulting Agreement

Each template should include in-line `[FIELD]` placeholders and
counsel-review markers (`⚠ counsel review required`) at decision points.

Source templates from your firm's library; do NOT include paywalled
form-book content without licensing.

The pack installer ingests every file in this directory.
