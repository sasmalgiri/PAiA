# Clinical content slot — Evidence-Based Protocols

This document is a content placeholder. Before publishing PAiA Therapy v1.0.0,
drop the following primary-source documents in this directory, organised
by modality.

## Required (priority 1) — open-licence content that can ship

- **PCIT (Parent-Child Interaction Therapy)** — UNC PCIT International
  publishes clinician training materials with permissible-use
  licences for clinical teams. https://www.pcit.org
- **TF-CBT (Trauma-Focused CBT)** — the TF-CBT Web (medicaluniversitysc)
  modules are free for clinicians; the published clinician's manual
  (Cohen, Mannarino, Deblinger) is paid.
- **CPT (Cognitive Processing Therapy)** — VA/DoD publishes CPT
  materials free for VA-affiliated clinicians; the Resick clinician's
  manual is paid; public-domain treatment-fidelity rubrics exist.
- **Beck Institute open modules** — Beck Institute provides several
  free clinician-training modules on CBT for depression, anxiety,
  panic, and CBT-I.
- **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline clinician resources** — public
  domain safety-planning templates (Stanley-Brown SPI is free for
  clinical use; cite the developers).
- **Columbia Suicide Severity Rating Scale (C-SSRS)** — free clinician
  version available from the Columbia Lighthouse Project.
- **PHQ-9, GAD-7, ORS/SRS** — Pfizer / Lambert public-domain
  measurement tools.
- **TIP series (Treatment Improvement Protocols)** — SAMHSA / HHS
  public-domain comprehensive treatment manuals for SUD, integrated
  care, trauma-informed care.

## Required (priority 1) — paid manuals that must be licensed

Cite only; do NOT ship verbatim. Cross-reference for clinicians who
have purchased.

- DBT skills training manual (Linehan, 2nd ed.) and DBT skills
  training handouts (Linehan, 2nd ed.) — Guilford Press
- DBT-A (adolescent) materials — Miller, Rathus, Linehan — Guilford
  Press
- EMDR Basic Training materials — EMDRIA-approved providers; cannot
  ship
- Gottman Method materials — Gottman Institute; paid
- EFT (Greenberg / Johnson) clinician's manual — paid
- Coping Cat (child anxiety CBT) and FRIENDS (child anxiety) — paid

## Recommended (priority 2) — clinical decision trees

- **APA Clinical Practice Guidelines** — free; depression, PTSD,
  geriatric depression
- **NICE Guidelines (UK)** — free; cover most mental-health
  presentations with treatment-recommendation summaries
- **VA/DoD Clinical Practice Guidelines** — free; PTSD, MDD, SUD, SMI
- **ASAM Criteria** for level-of-care decisions in SUD — paid; cite
  only

## Quality checklist before publishing

- [ ] Clinical content reviewer with relevant modality training has
      signed off (e.g. DBT-LBC certified clinician reviews DBT content;
      EMDRIA-certified clinician reviews EMDR content)
- [ ] Every paid manual is REFERENCED ONLY, not reproduced
- [ ] Every protocol has explicit scope limitations (which populations,
      which presentations, contraindications)
- [ ] Crisis-resource frames are present in every protocol section
- [ ] Cultural-adaptation considerations are included where applicable
