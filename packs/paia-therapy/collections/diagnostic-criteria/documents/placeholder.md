# Clinical content slot — Diagnostic Criteria

This document is a content placeholder. Before publishing PAiA Therapy v1.0.0,
drop the following primary-source documents in this directory:

## Required (priority 1)

- **DSM-5-TR criteria summaries.** Cannot ship verbatim — DSM-5-TR is APA
  copyrighted. Options:
  - Paraphrase under fair-use for clinical reference (with attribution)
  - License authoritative content from APA (Professional licensing)
  - Cross-reference to publicly available criteria summaries from
    sources like Merck Manual Professional, MSD Manual, peer-reviewed
    open-access review articles
- **Differential diagnosis decision trees** — these are CLINICIAN-created
  workflow documents (multiple authors publish these in textbooks and
  open clinical-training materials).
- **Cultural Formulation Interview (CFI)** — DSM-5-TR Appendix; APA
  provides the CFI as a free PDF on their site for clinical use.

## Recommended (priority 2)

- **ICD-10-CM / ICD-11 mental-and-behavioural disorders chapter** — WHO
  public domain. See the ICD Codes collection for full ICD content;
  put diagnostic-grouping summaries here for differential reasoning.
- **Common-comorbidity matrices** — published clinical reviews of
  co-occurring conditions (substance use × mood, ADHD × anxiety,
  trauma × dissociation, eating disorders × OCD, etc.).
- **Culturally-informed disambiguators** — e.g. distinguishing
  Susto / Nervios / Mal de Ojo from DSM disorders; trauma presentations
  in refugee populations; spiritual-experience vs psychosis frameworks.

## Quality checklist before publishing

- [ ] Clinical content reviewer (LMFT, LCSW, PsyD, or psychiatrist) has
      signed off on every file in this directory
- [ ] DSM-5-TR copyrighted content is paraphrased, not verbatim, OR
      licensed from APA with attribution
- [ ] Each diagnostic category includes the standard 'specifier' options
      that clinicians need to document accurately
- [ ] Disambiguators for adjacent diagnoses are present (e.g.
      bipolar II vs cyclothymia vs persistent depressive disorder)
- [ ] No specific patient cases or de-identified-but-recognisable
      material from training materials

The pack installer ingests every file in this directory into the
embedded collection.
